Architecture portfolio with a 3D hero

A portfolio for an architect

A personal site for an architect. As you scroll, a 3D house turns, splits into its four layers, then lifts the upper floors away to show the ground plan. Her photo stands inside the model as the human for scale. After that: a project gallery that tilts in 3D, her quote revealed word by word, and a downloadable lookbook.

  • React and Vite, with the 3D built in Three.js (React Three Fiber)
  • The house is generated in code, not a downloaded model
  • The 3D part loads only when needed, so the page stays fast
  • Gallery scrolls on desktop and swipes on phones

Private project, so there is no public link. I can walk you through it live on a call.

Private app for two, installs on a phone

A movie night app for a couple

Two people who can never decide what to watch each swipe on movies. When both like the same one, the app shows "It's a match" on both phones at once and adds it to tonight's shortlist. It also tracks what they are watching, shows shared taste stats, and has an AI helper that turns a plain request ("something funny, under two hours") into picks with a reason for each.

  • Next.js app that installs to the home screen like a native app
  • Supabase for login and a shared database; both phones update in about 1.4 seconds
  • Push notifications sent from the database itself, so they arrive even on bad networks
  • Each person chooses which alerts they get

Private project, so there is no public link. I can walk you through it live on a call.

Personal tool: data pipeline with AI

A job portal that reads the job market for me

Every morning and evening it pulls new remote jobs from public job feeds and Telegram channels, keeps only jobs open to someone in Ethiopia, and scores each one against my CVs. It flags stale and fake-looking posts, shows which skills the best jobs ask for that my CV lacks, and drafts a tailored cover letter and CV for a job in the background.

  • Python (FastAPI) with HTMX pages and a SQLite database
  • Runs on a schedule twice a day
  • AI drafting runs in a background queue, so the page never freezes
  • Tailored CVs come out as clean PDFs

Private project, so there is no public link. I can walk you through it live on a call.
